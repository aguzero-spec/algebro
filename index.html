Welcome to Lesson Packet Builder

Give this app a lesson outline or a lesson plan you already have, choose a few options, and it writes a full lesson packet: a detailed lesson plan, student worksheet, quiz with answer key, vocabulary list, and versions for struggling and advanced learners. You can edit everything, save it, and export it to Word, PDF, or PowerPoint.

The writing is done by Claude, an AI from Anthropic. The app uses your own Claude account, so any cost is billed to you by Anthropic, usually well under a dollar per packet. Your packets stay on this computer.

Step 1: Add your Claude API key

  1. Go to platform.claude.com and sign up or log in.
  2. Add some credit under Billing (for example $10).
  3. Open API Keys, click Create Key, name it (e.g. “Lesson Packet Builder”), and copy it.
  4. Paste it below. It will be stored in your Mac's Keychain, not in a file.

Step 2: Set a monthly spending limit

A limit stops charges from ever going higher than you expect.

  1. At platform.claude.com, open Settings › Limits.
  2. Under Spend limits, click Set spend limit (or Change Limit).
  3. Enter a monthly amount you're comfortable with, such as $10, and save.

Tip: if you pay with prepaid credits, leave auto-reload off under Billing; then you can never spend more than the credit you added.

Do not enter student names or student records. Your lesson outline and options are sent to Claude to write the packet.

1. Your lesson outline

A rough plan or an existing lesson. Claude builds the full packet from it. Type or paste it below, or upload a file; uploaded text appears in the box so you can check it.

What should I include?

Anything from a few lines to a couple of pages works. The more of your own lesson you include, the more closely the packet follows it. Most helpful, in this order:

  1. The goal: what students should understand or be able to do.
  2. The main activities, one line each, with rough timing (and which day, if more than one).
  3. Anything to keep exactly: specific problems, texts, examples, or vocabulary.
  4. Required routines: your district's lesson cycle (e.g. HISD NES), warm-ups, exit tickets.
  5. How students show what they learned: exit ticket, project, quiz.

No need to repeat the grade, TEKS, number of days, or class notes: those go in the boxes below. Leave out student names.

Example outline:

Topic: Dividing fractions by fractions
Goal: Students understand why dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal, and divide fractions and mixed numbers fluently.

Day 1
- Warm-up (5 min): three problems multiplying fractions
- Model (15 min): "How many 1/4s are in 3?" with fraction strips; connect it to 3 × 4
- Partner practice (20 min): six problems, first with models, then with the rule
- Exit ticket (10 min): 2/3 ÷ 1/6 and one word problem

Day 2
- Mixed numbers: change to improper fractions first (2 1/2 ÷ 3/4)
- Word problems with recipes and ribbon lengths
- Review game, then a short quiz

Keep: the fraction strips, and the problem "How many 3/4-cup scoops are in 6 cups of flour?"
Students show learning by explaining one problem two ways: with a model and with the rule.
.docx, .pdf, .txt, or .md

Saved packets are kept in this browser on this computer. Clearing your browser's data (or using another computer or browser) means they're not there, so download a backup now and then. Claude settings

Sections

The sections a packet can have, in order. Ticked ones are selected for new packets. A new section gets its own prompt (below) for you to fill in.

Prompts

The instructions sent to Claude. Placeholders in double braces are filled in from your form, e.g. {{seed}} (your lesson outline), {{grade_level}}, {{teks}}, {{lesson_plan}}. Your edits apply to the next section you generate, and app updates never replace them.

Prompts and settings files

The instructions sent to Claude are plain text files you can edit with TextEdit. Changes apply the next time you generate. config.yaml (model, length, prices, Word styling) and sections.yaml (which sections exist) are in the data folder.

About

Lesson Packet Builder turns an existing lesson plan into a full lesson packet using Claude. Everything is stored on this computer; the only thing sent out is the request to Claude.

This is the web version: it runs in your browser, with no server of its own. Your key, outline, and packets go only to Claude (api.anthropic.com), straight from this browser. Prefer an app? The Mac and Windows versions do the same.

Texas edition. Packets are aligned to the TEKS, with the official TEA wording built in for Grade 6–8 Mathematics (including the 2025 Advanced Mathematics courses), ELPS (adopted 2024) supports for emergent bilingual students, STAAR-style quizzes, and optional Spanish support.
